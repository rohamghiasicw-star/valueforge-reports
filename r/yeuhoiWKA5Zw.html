What's holding the site back

AreaFinding
Links and internal linking Ten internal links return errors, including the pages at /electrician-markham/, /electrician-richmond-hill/ and /electrician-brampton/, all 404s, which means Google has nothing to rank for those cities. Another 20 links point nowhere, href='#' placeholders on items like Services and Features, and 10 main calls to action send clicks off-site, for example to Facebook, which leaks link authority away from the pages you want ranking. On top of that one crawled page has no H1 and one title tag is duplicated, so a few pages are competing with or undercutting each other.
Google profile alignment Your Google profile lists 416 797 4084, but the site shows 1 855 608 0178 and 1 855 923 5004. Mismatched name, address and phone data is one of the most common causes of a stalled map pack ranking, because Google can't confirm the listing and the website are the same business. There's also no Google map embed on the site, a standard local relevance signal that's easy to add.
Index control A sitemap exists at /sitemaps.xml listing 91 URLs, which is good. But the homepage has no canonical tag, so if URL variants of it exist Google can index more than one version and split ranking signals between them. One tag fixes it.
Error pages and dead URLs The WordPress login page at /wp-login.php returns a 200 and is reachable by crawlers. An indexable login page burns crawl budget on a URL that will never earn you a customer, and it advertises your admin door to automated attack scripts. It should be blocked from crawling and locked down.
On-page basics Your homepage title reads "Top-Rated Ontario Electrical Services: Power Blitz Experts" and contains neither electrician nor North York, the exact words the searches you want are built around. The title tag is the strongest on-page relevance signal a page has, so right now your best page isn't raising its hand for your best keyword. The meta description is present at the right length and Open Graph tags are set, so shared links preview correctly.
Images and speed 18 images are served as PNG with no WebP versions, and converting them is a direct mobile load speed win, which feeds both rankings and conversions. The homepage HTML transfers 244 KB and responded in 929 ms, so there's room to tighten. Two of 34 images are missing alt text, small but easy relevance and accessibility points left on the table.
HTTPS and redirects HTTPS is enforced sitewide and assets load securely. That's a baseline trust signal for both Google and visitors, and it's done right.
Review and trust signals Trust signals are in place on the site. That supports conversion once the local visibility issues above are fixed, so the traffic you win actually turns into calls.
Structure and content The homepage carries 1047 words of crawlable copy, and Electrician, FAQPage, Organization and WebSite schema are all present. That structured data tells Google exactly what kind of business this is, and most trade sites never get this far. The foundation is genuinely good.

The pages that should win you Markham, Richmond Hill and Brampton searches return 404s, and your Google profile phone number doesn't match your site, which is exactly what stalls a map pack ranking.

The fix, in priority order

WhenWhat happens
Week 1Rebuild or 301 the 404'd Markham, Richmond Hill and Brampton pages, align the Google profile phone number with the site so NAP data matches everywhere, and embed a Google map. Stop the local bleed first.
Weeks 1-2Set a canonical tag on the homepage, rewrite the title tag around electrician North York, resolve the duplicated title, add the missing H1, and block and secure /wp-login.php so crawlers stop wasting budget on it.
Weeks 2-4Convert the 18 PNG images to WebP, add the two missing alt texts, repair or remove the 20 placeholder links, and reroute the 10 off-site calls to action to your own service pages so link authority stays where it earns rankings.

What clean SEO is worth

When someone in North York needs an electrician, they search, scan the map pack, and call from whatever page Google shows them. Right now Google is being told two different phone numbers for your business, so it hesitates to show you there at all. And when it looks for your page about Markham, Richmond Hill or Brampton, it finds a 404. Fixing the profile mismatch and rebuilding those city pages puts you in front of the exact searches that turn into service calls, with the 1047 words of homepage copy and the Electrician schema you already have doing the supporting work.
