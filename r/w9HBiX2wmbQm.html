What's holding the site back

AreaFinding
No clear index control The site serves no robots.txt, no XML sitemap at any standard location, and no canonical tag on the homepage. That means Google gets no crawl directives, no map of your 12 pages and no signal about which version of a page is the real one. Indexation of the whole site is being left to chance.
Broken and placeholder links 13 of your main call to action links send clicks off-site, for example "visit facebook profile" goes to facebook.com/4KESNOW, which leaks link authority to Facebook instead of the pages you want ranking. The service area list naming Barrie, Innisfil, Alcona, Midhurst, Minesing, Thornton and Angus is a dead link pointing nowhere, so the element that should feed local area pages goes nowhere and reads as unfinished to visitors and to Google. One title tag is also duplicated across pages, which makes those pages compete with each other for the same search.
Title, meta and heading structure The homepage title is 46 characters, "Kinetic Electric Services | Barrie Electrician", and never mentions snow or removal. The title tag is the strongest single relevance signal on a page, so Google is told this is an electrician's site and ranks it that way. The meta description runs 187 characters and gets cut off mid sentence, and only 5 H2 headings means the page is thin on the subheadings Google reads to work out what you actually cover.
Internal linking Every page links to nearly every other page, a flat structure rather than a hierarchy. Nothing tells Google which pages matter most, and a site that ranks has a shape, homepage, then service pages, then the areas under each. Right now all 12 pages carry equal weight, so none of them carries enough.
Review and trust signals There is no enquiry form on the homepage, so every lead has to phone or email. For snow removal, where people search at night and in bad weather, that means losing everyone who was ready to fill in a form but not ready to call.
Structure and content The homepage carries 550 words, well under the 1500 to 2500 a page built to rank for a service runs, because that is what it takes to answer a buyer's questions before they call. There is also no schema markup anywhere, so search engines have to infer what the business is instead of being told.
Google profile alignment The site has no LocalBusiness schema, so your name, address and phone are never stated in a machine-readable way, and there is no Google map embed, a standard local relevance signal. The name on your Google profile, KE Snow, does not appear on the site itself. Google matches your profile to your site by exactly these signals, and right now the two do not confirm each other.
HTTPS and redirects HTTPS is enforced sitewide and all assets load securely. That is a trust and ranking baseline many local sites still get wrong, and yours is clean.
Error pages and dead URLs Dead URLs return a proper 404 and no error pages are exposed. Google is not wasting crawl budget on junk pages, which is exactly what you want.
Images and speed The homepage HTML transfers just 31 KB and responded in 777 ms. That is a lean, fast base, so the rebuild work below starts from a page that already loads well.

Your homepage title still reads "Kinetic Electric Services | Barrie Electrician", so Google is filing kesnow.ca as an electrician site, not a snow removal company.
