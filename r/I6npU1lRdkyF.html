What's holding the site back

AreaFinding
No clear index control The site serves no robots.txt, no XML sitemap and no canonical tag on the homepage. That means Google gets zero crawl directives and no map of your 12 pages, so it discovers and interprets the site entirely on its own. For a local business competing on electrician Barrie, that is slower indexation and rankings decided by guesswork instead of by you.
Broken links and off-site call to action leaks Your service areas link listing Barrie, Innisfil, Alcona, Midhurst, Minesing, Thornton, Angus and New Tecumseth points nowhere, which reads as an unfinished site to visitors and to Google. Worse, 13 main call to action links send clicks straight off-site, for example 'visit facebook profile' goes to facebook.com/4KESNOW, which leaks link authority to Facebook instead of building it on the pages you want ranking. One title tag is also duplicated across pages, so two pages compete for the same search.
Title, meta and heading structure The homepage title runs 46 characters, 'Kinetic Electric Services | Barrie Electrician', which names the city but leaves the service and qualifier space unused. The meta description is 187 characters and gets cut off mid sentence in results, which costs clicks from people who never read your pitch. The page has exactly one H1, which is right, but only 5 H2 headings, so Google has few subheadings to read what you actually cover.
Internal linking Every page links to nearly every other page, a flat structure rather than a hierarchy. Nothing tells Google which pages are the important ones, so link authority spreads evenly instead of concentrating on the services you want ranking. A site that ranks has a shape: homepage, then service pages, then the areas under each.
Structure and content The homepage carries 550 words, and a page built to rank for a service runs 1500 to 2500, because that is what it takes to answer a buyer's questions before they call. The site also has no schema markup, so search engines have to infer what the business is instead of being told.
Google profile alignment There is no LocalBusiness or Organization schema on the site, so your name, address and phone are never stated in a machine-readable way that ties the site to your Google profile. There is also no Google map embed, a standard local relevance signal. Both gaps make it harder for Google to trust that kesnow.ca is the Barrie electrician it should show in the map pack.
Review and trust signals There is no enquiry form on the homepage, so every lead has to phone or email. Anyone browsing after hours or from a job site who does not want to call has no way to reach you, and those leads go to the next electrician who gives them a form.
Mixed http and https HTTPS is enforced sitewide and all assets load securely. That is a trust and ranking baseline many local sites still get wrong, and yours is clean.
Live error pages exposed Dead URLs return a proper 404 and no error pages are exposed. Google is not wasting crawl budget on broken routes and nothing embarrassing is indexed.
Images and speed The homepage HTML transfers just 31 KB and responded in 301 ms. That is a lean, fast base, which means the fixes above land on a site that already loads well instead of one that needs a performance rescue first.

kesnow.ca gives Google no sitemap, no robots.txt and no canonical tags, so your shot at ranking for electrician Barrie is left to guesswork.

What clean SEO is worth

When someone in Barrie needs an electrician, they search once, call one or two of the results, and hire whoever answers. Right now your site is fast and secure but effectively unmapped for Google, and your strongest buttons hand clicks to Facebook. Fixing indexation, keeping clicks on your own service pages, and giving each of the eight towns you serve a page of its own puts Kinetic Electric in front of those searches instead of behind them. Adding an enquiry form means the leads that do arrive after hours actually reach you.
