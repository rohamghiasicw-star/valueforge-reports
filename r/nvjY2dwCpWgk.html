What's holding the site back

AreaFinding
Title, meta and heading structure Your homepage has no H1, so nothing on the page states its main subject, and the title tag, while a healthy 79 characters, does not contain the word house. Searches for your service are built around that word, so Google has to guess what the page is about. The meta description is present and correctly sized at 163 characters and you have 7 H2 headings in the right band, so the frame is there. It is just missing the two signals that matter most.
Index control Your sitemap is live at /sitemap.xml with 13 URLs, which is good, but the homepage carries no canonical tag. Without one, any URL variation Google finds gets treated as a separate page, which splits ranking signals that should all point at one address.
HTTPS and redirects One image still loads over plain http on your https pages, a Squarespace-hosted jpg. Mixed content like this triggers browser warnings and chips away at the secure-site signal Google uses, for the sake of one URL that takes minutes to fix.
Links and internal linking Five of the 11 pages crawled have no H1 and two title tags are duplicated across pages. Every page without an H1 is a page Google has to guess at, and duplicated titles make two pages compete for the same search instead of each owning its own. That flattens the whole site's ability to rank service by service.
Review and trust signals There is no enquiry form on the homepage and no tap-to-call link, so every lead has to manually phone or email. On mobile, where most local cleaning searches happen, each extra step loses people who would have converted with one tap.
Images and speed 18 images are served as jpg or png with no WebP versions, and the homepage HTML transfers 433 KB. Converting those images to compressed WebP is a direct mobile load-speed win, and load speed is both a ranking factor and the difference between a visitor waiting or leaving.
Structure and content The homepage carries 751 words. A page built to rank for a service runs 1500 to 2500, because that is what it takes to answer the questions a buyer has before they call, and yours is well under that. The only schema present is WebSite, and there is no FAQPage schema, which is worth adding for the question coverage itself since those answers are content Google can rank you for.
Google profile alignment The phone number on your site matches your Google profile exactly, which is the right start. But there is no LocalBusiness schema, so the site never states its name, address and phone in a machine-readable way, and there is no Google map embed, a standard local relevance signal. For a Calgary service business, those two gaps weaken the link between your website and your map listing.
Error pages and dead URLs Dead URLs return a proper 404 and no error pages are exposed. That means no broken pages leaking into the index and no crawl budget wasted on junk, which is cleaner than most sites we audit.
Internal linking The 10 pages crawled are properly wired. Nothing is orphaned, the homepage feeds the deeper pages and they link back, so once the headings and titles are fixed, link authority will already flow where it should.

Your homepage never tells Google it is about house cleaning, no H1 and a title without the word house means the page you most need ranking is invisible for the exact search Calgary customers type.

The site is already well built and well linked. It just is not saying the words that get it found.
