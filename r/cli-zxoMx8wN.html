What's holding the site back

AreaFinding
No clear index control There is no XML sitemap at /sitemap.xml or /sitemap_index.xml and none referenced in robots.txt, and the homepage carries no canonical tag. A sitemap is how you tell Google which pages exist and matter, and canonicals are how you tell it which version of each page to rank. Without either, Google decides your indexation on its own, and it does not always decide in your favour.
Broken and placeholder links Of 61 links checked across 12 pages, 4 return errors or never resolve, including the Ontario home electrical safety page (404), and 20 more point nowhere at all, placeholder '#' links behind 'Menu', 'Service Areas', 'Search' and 'Back to top', which read as an unfinished site to visitors and to Google. On top of that, 12 main calls to action send clicks straight off-site, for example to facebook.com/TorontoWiring, leaking link authority away from the service pages you want ranking. One title tag is also duplicated across pages, which blurs which page should rank for which search.
Review and trust signals The site marks up its own star rating in schema. Self-serving review markup is against Google's structured data policy, and the penalty when it is caught is losing rich results sitewide, exactly the stars that make a local electrician stand out on the results page. Better to remove it on your own terms than have it pulled.
Exposed login path /wp-login.php is live and returning a 200 to anyone who asks, crawlers included. Search engines can index it alongside your real pages, and an openly exposed WordPress login is also a standing invitation to automated break-in attempts. It should be hidden or locked down.
Images and speed 9 images are served as PNG with no WebP versions, and 3 of 19 images have no alt text, which is a missed relevance signal and an accessibility gap. Converting those PNGs to compressed WebP is a direct mobile load-speed win, and speed is both a ranking and a conversion factor for someone searching for an electrician from their phone. The base is quick, the homepage HTML is 175 KB and responded in 293 ms, so this is polish rather than surgery.
Local schema and map signals There is no LocalBusiness or Organization schema anywhere on the site, so it never states your name, address and phone in a machine-readable way, and there is no Google map embed. Both are standard signals Google uses to tie a website to a place and a Business Profile. For a business that wants to own electrician etobicoke, that connection is the whole game.
HTTPS and redirects HTTPS is enforced and every asset loads securely. That is a trust and ranking box already ticked, nothing to do here.
Title and meta in place The title tag is present and well sized, "Toronto Wiring-Electrician in Toronto-Electrical Contractor", the meta description is the right length, and Open Graph tags are set so shared links preview correctly. The on-page basics have been done properly.
Structure and content The homepage carries 1,786 words of crawlable copy, more than most trade sites manage, which gives Google real text to rank. Two easy upgrades sit here: the only schema type found is Book, which tells Google nothing about an electrical contractor, and there is no FAQPage schema, the cheapest way to pick up extra SERP real estate.

Google has no map of torontowiring.com, no sitemap and no canonical tags, so it is guessing which pages should rank for electrician etobicoke.
